================================================================================
COLUMN ALIASES
================================================================================

The CSV parser recognizes these alternative column names:

  selling_price  →  unit_price  (use if your CSV says "selling_price")
  sale_price     →  unit_price  (use if your CSV says "sale_price")
  price          →  unit_price  (use if your CSV says "price")
  cost_price     →  unit_cost   (use if your CSV says "cost_price")
  cost           →  unit_cost   (use if your CSV says "cost")

Example: If your CSV has "selling_price" instead of "unit_price", it will
automatically be converted. No need to rename columns!

================================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING
================================================================================

Q: "Could not find the 'category' column"
A: Migration hasn't been run yet. Follow steps 1-3 above.

Q: "Missing required columns: ..."
A: Check your CSV has these (aliases OK): product_name, sku, sale_date,
   quantity, unit_price (or alias), unit_cost (or alias)

Q: "Column 'X' must be numeric"
A: Check numeric columns only contain numbers:
   - discount: 2.50
   - supplier_cost: 12.00
   - customer_rating: 4.5 (on 1-5 scale)
   - returns: 2 (whole number)
   - inventory_level: 85 (whole number)
