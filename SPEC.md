# Project Name

BusinessPilot AI - An AI Business Co-Pilot for Small Businesses

---

# Problem

Small business owners spend hours manually tracking sales, profits, inventory, and product performance through spreadsheets. This process is repetitive, time-consuming, and often delays important business decisions.

---

# Target Users

- Small business owners
- Retail shop owners
- E-commerce sellers

---

# Core Flow

Login
→ Connect sales/inventory data
→ AI analyzes business performance
→ Dashboard displays KPIs
→ AI explains profit/loss trends
→ AI predicts demand
→ AI recommends actions

---

# Must Have

- Track sales, revenue, profit and loss
- Monitor inventory
- Identify best and worst selling products
- Explain reasons behind sales decline
- AI-generated business insights
- Dashboard with key metrics

---

# Should Have

- Demand forecasting
- Weekly and monthly reports
- Alerts for low inventory
- Sales trend visualization
- Compare performance over time

---

# Could Have

- Marketing suggestions
- Smart restocking recommendations
- AI chatbot for business questions
- Email or WhatsApp notifications
- Integration with Shopify/POS systems
